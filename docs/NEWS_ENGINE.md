# Lighter News Intelligence

Lighter treats news as adversarial, time-sensitive data. Raw headlines are evidence, not trading instructions.

## Processing stages

1. Ingest
2. Validate
3. Normalize
4. Deduplicate
5. Establish provenance
6. Extract entities
7. Resolve assets
8. Classify event
9. Estimate materiality
10. Estimate novelty
11. Estimate source confidence
12. Compare independent sources
13. Detect contradictions
14. Measure market reaction
15. Generate a versioned signal
16. Apply risk gates

## Important distinctions

- Fact vs opinion
- Announcement vs rumor
- New information vs repeated information
- Direct impact vs narrative spillover
- Positive language vs positive economic impact
- Initial reaction vs sustained reaction
- News-driven move vs unrelated market move

## Anti-hype controls

Social engagement, follower counts, headline capitalization, emotional wording, and raw sentiment intensity must not independently create a trade signal.

## Event lifecycle

`observed -> validated -> enriched -> clustered -> scored -> confirmed -> tradable -> expired`

Critical corrections/retractions transition an event to `invalidated` and invalidate derived trade intents that have not already completed execution.
